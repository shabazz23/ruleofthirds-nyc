About

One roof,
every setup.

Golden Hour Loft is a 5,000 sq ft Brooklyn production space under Rule of Thirds, built for the way modern shoots actually run — product and e-commerce, branded content, short films, podcasts, and social, all in one day.

The clean backdrops, styled kitchen, lounge, and private rooftop give you versatile, on-brand sets without leaving the building. Shoot a full catalog, or a week of posts, before the light changes.
